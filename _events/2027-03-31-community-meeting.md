Join the European lattice field theory community at [CERN][cern]
to discuss our computing future and the opportunities offered by AI.

- EuroLFT Community Meeting · 31 March–1 April (morning)
  The meeting will address community priorities,
  European HPC and AI infrastructure,
  and future computing needs,
  with a session for emerging researchers.
- Lattice Field Theory and AI · 1 April (afternoon)–2 April
  The workshop will explore AI in lattice field theory and related sciences,
  including a community discussion of the AI white paper.

The programme is in preparation.
[Registration][indico] is expected to open in mid-November 2026,
and there is no registration fee.

For more information,
see [the event information on Indico][indico]
